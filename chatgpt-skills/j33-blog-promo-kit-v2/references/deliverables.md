# Deliverables

Use exact user-supplied dimensions when provided. Otherwise use these defaults.

| File | Purpose | Size | Ratio / format |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `instagram.png` | Instagram feed photo | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 PNG |
| `linkedin.png` | LinkedIn image post | 1200 × 627 | 1.91:1 PNG |
| `x.png` | X image post | 1600 × 900 | 16:9 PNG |
| `j33-banner.webp` | J33.AI article hero/banner | 1672 × 941 | 16:9 WebP |
| `j33-thumbnail.webp` | J33.AI blog listing/card | 800 × 600 | 4:3 WebP |
| `post-copy.md` | Ready-to-paste social copy | — | UTF-8 Markdown |

## Safe zones

- Keep critical text and the logo at least 6% from every edge.
- On the Instagram image, keep the main subject away from the lower 18%, where interface overlays may compete.
- Make the website thumbnail understandable when displayed at approximately 320 px wide.
- Use a faithful short title of roughly 3–8 words on the thumbnail when the article title is too long.

## Delivery check

- All five images share the same core concept, palette and typographic system.
- Title spelling and capitalization match the article.
- No unintended text, watermark or invented logo appears in the artwork.
- No faces, hands, cables, screens, code or diagrams contain visible generation defects.
- Each file has the required pixel dimensions and opens successfully.
- Image text is sharp at 100%, uses a neutral high-contrast colour, and remains readable on mobile.
- `post-copy.md` contains separate Instagram, LinkedIn and X sections.
